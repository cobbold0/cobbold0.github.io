# DataWise Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 5, 2026**

## Summary (in plain English)

DataWise reads how much internet data each app on your phone uses, so it can show it to you, forecast your month and warn you about limits you set. **All of that stays on your phone. We never see it, and it is never uploaded.** The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads. You can remove ads with the one-time Premium purchase.

## What the app reads, and why

With your permission (**Usage access**, which you turn on in Android's settings), DataWise reads from Android:

- How much mobile data and Wi-Fi each app and the phone as a whole have used (Android's network statistics)
- How often each app was opened (Android's usage statistics), to spot apps that use a lot of data but are rarely used
- The names and icons of your installed apps, to show which app is which

This information is processed **only on your device**. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

## Data stored on your device

DataWise stores on your device:

- The data limits you set and which limit notifications were already sent
- Whether you bought Premium (so ads stay off)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Notifications

If you set a data limit, DataWise checks your usage about every 15 minutes in the background and notifies you at 80%, 100% and 125% of the limit. This happens entirely on your device.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads from **Google AdMob** on some screens. The main Overview screen and notifications never show ads.

To show, personalize and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

- Your device's advertising ID
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device and app information (for example, device model, OS version, app version)
- Ad interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped)

We do not receive this information in a way that identifies you. Learn how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices:**

- **EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland:** you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
- **Everyone:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
- You can remove all ads with the one-time **Premium** purchase.

## In-app purchase (Premium)

The optional **Premium** purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so Premium stays active if you reinstall. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

1. **Usage access:** to read data used per app and how often apps are opened (see above). You turn it on yourself in Settings and can turn it off at any time.
2. **See all installed apps:** to show the names and icons of the apps that use data.
3. **Notifications:** to warn you about data limits you set.
4. **Internet:** used only to show ads and check your Premium purchase. Your usage data is never sent anywhere.

DataWise does **not** access your contacts, messages, location, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it cannot see what you do inside other apps, only how much data they use.

## Children's privacy

DataWise is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your control over your data

You can:

- Turn off Usage access for DataWise in Android's settings at any time
- Remove any limit you set
- Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
- Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. We do not hold personal data about you. For data processed by Google for advertising, see https://policies.google.com/privacy or contact Google.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
